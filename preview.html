Three tiers, one source

Every value on this page is generated from the same JSON that imports into Figma — primitives hold raw values, semantic tokens assign them jobs, and component tokens compose those roles. Each specimen shows its full resolution chain, so you can trace any colour back to the primitive it came from.

Brand

Where the identity is decided. Everything downstream points here.

The brand ramp is the only place a hue is chosen for its own sake. Step 600 carries solid actions in light mode, 500 in dark — every other brand decision aliases one of those two.
